Three directions, each shown in both themes, at large size, at favicon sizes and in the header lockup.

A. Pad J Recommended

A 4 by 4 grid of square pads. The lit pads draw a J. It reads as a drum machine pad bank, a pixel letter and a rack of services at the same time, and it still works at 16 px.

B. Signal path

Four rows of pads stand for four layers of a system. One pad lights per row and a trace connects them, so the mark is the positioning itself: one decision traveling through every layer.

C. Wordmark

Initials in the pixel display font with an amber cursor block. The simplest to apply anywhere, and the least distinctive.
